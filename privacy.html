Century Desk Uploader - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Century Desk Uploader ("the tool") is a private command-line application operated by the owner of the YouTube channel The Century Desk. It runs only on the owner's personal computer and is used only to upload and schedule videos on that one channel. It has no other users and no public interface.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube data is handled according to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • The OAuth authorization of the channel owner's own Google account (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube).
  • The IDs, upload status and privacy/publish status of videos the tool itself uploaded, and the owner's own playlist list (to add a new episode to the owner's playlist).

The tool does not access, collect or process data about any other YouTube user, viewer or channel.

How data is used and stored

  • The OAuth refresh token is stored in a local file on the owner's computer and is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos.
  • Video IDs and scheduled publish times of the owner's own uploads are kept in a local log file.
  • No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties. No analytics, advertising or tracking is performed. No cookies are used.

Retention and deletion

Local records are kept only as long as the channel is operated with the tool. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings (third-party access), after which the stored token no longer works and is deleted.
